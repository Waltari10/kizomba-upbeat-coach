What is “dancing on the up-beat”?

In Kizomba your body moves softly up and down with every step. Dancing on the up-beat means your body is at its highest on the beat and you sink (bend the knees) on the “and” in between. Dancing on the down-beat is the opposite: you drop into the floor on the beat. The coach measures how your hips move relative to the beat of the music and shows which one you're doing.
